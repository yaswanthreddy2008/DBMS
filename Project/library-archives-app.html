WHAT YOU CAN DO

Everything a membership desk does — in one app.

Built around the actions members repeat most often, each designed to work with a slow connection or none at all.

Member onboarding

Register once to create a digital library card, complete with profile and notification preferences.

Book search & management

Search the full catalogue by title, author, or tag, browse by category, and check live availability.

Asset management

Browse non-book archival assets and check their availability alongside the general catalogue.

Reservations

Hold an available title, reserve an asset, or cancel a reservation the moment plans change.

Borrowing history

A complete, searchable record of current and previous borrowing, always current.

Fine tracking

See what's owed and its payment status, updated the moment the backend recalculates it.

Offline access

A local cache keeps your last sync available with no connection at all.

Background synchronization

Local and server data reconcile automatically the moment a connection is available.

Push notifications

Due-date reminders, reservation updates, and library announcements, delivered in real time.
